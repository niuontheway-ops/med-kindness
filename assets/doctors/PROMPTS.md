# Doctor posture assets

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project. Reference: doctor-listen.webp.

Shared prompt: preserve the same doctor identity, hair bun, white coat, teal shirt, burgundy stethoscope and warm 3D cartoon style. Full body; head, torso and gaze toward image left at the other person's eye level. No text. Standing variants have no chair; seated-urgent remains seated.

- standing-listen: attentive listening, relaxed hands with clipboard.
- standing-empathy: concerned face, open hands acknowledging distress.
- standing-explain: calm explanation with an open palm and clipboard.
- standing-urgent: focused expression indicating the immediate next step.
- standing-boundary: calm, firm open-palm boundary gesture.
- seated-urgent: serious assessment while seated with clipboard.

Generated raster backgrounds were removed during asset preparation; transparent WebP files are supplied at desktop and mobile sizes. Original generated PNGs are retained locally. Sitting/standing is chosen from the other character's actual posture; all dialogue actions keep that posture.
